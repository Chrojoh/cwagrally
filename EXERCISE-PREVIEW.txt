First-person exercise preview — C-WAGS Starter

Open the app over HTTP using start.cmd (Python required), or run
npx serve . -l 8080 with Node.js, then open http://localhost:8080.
Choose a course and click Walk course. Perform exercises is enabled by
default. Play walks between stations and runs supported handler movements.
Pause at signs stops AFTER a supported exercise. Play/Pause also pauses
mid-exercise. Station buttons and the slider reset the exercise preview.

SUPPORTED C-WAGS SIGNS
S1/S2: right and left turns
S3/S4: full 270-degree right/left rotations
S5/S6: halt, right pivot, halt/forward
S7/S8: right/left about turns
S13: one, two, three forward steps with halts
S17: one, two, three backward steps with gaze held forward
S18/S19/S20: slow, fast, normal travel pace
S21: forward diagonal side step right
S22/S31: Zig and Zag, opposing right/left turn sequences
S23/S24: complete right/left 360-degree circles
S30: halt
S32/S33: approximately 45-degree bearing changes

Source: https://c-wags.org/wp-content/uploads/2021/04/Starter.2021-web.pdf
The linked 2021 descriptions match this app's historical C-WAGS pack.
These are illustrative HANDLER movement previews. No dog is displayed.
Step length, radius and timing are design choices, not official dimensions.
Spirals, serpentine, figure eights and other unsupported signs are labelled
as having no motion script. Other organizations remain route previews.
The animation does not validate or certify exercise compliance or working
space. It does not collision-check its added movement paths.

TESTS
Passed: full signed 270/360 rotations; stationary pivot coordinates;
backing position and fixed gaze; opposite Zig/Zag offsets; coordinate
transforms; sampled motion continuity; route playback and camera smoothing;
exercise arrival, execution and continuous route rejoin. JavaScript syntax
and diff whitespace checks passed. Real-browser visual review remains needed.
