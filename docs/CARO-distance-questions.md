# CARO course-design distance clarification

Subject: Clarification of Rally course spacing and measurement points

Hello CARO,

I am working on a Rally course-design tool and would like to confirm how it should measure and check spacing. The Course Design Checklist (V12-2026 08) says “Correct distances between stations (generally 10’ or 15’ min).” We have reviewed the Rally Handbook 2025 v1.0, its December 14, 2025 amendments, the Trial Officials Guide effective January 1, 2026, the Virtual Trial Companion Guide v1.02, and the February 24, 2026 Rally design template.

Could you clarify the questions below, ideally with sign numbers, minimum distances, and the points between which each distance is measured? An existing judge-training handout or annotated course map would be very helpful.

## 1. Ordinary station spacing — highest priority

1. Which stations or station combinations require **10 feet**, and which require **15 feet**? Is there a list by sign number?
2. Does “generally” make these enforceable minimums, recommended targets, or distances that a course approver may vary? What exceptions are permitted?
3. Is spacing measured **sign to sign**, between the team's exercise starting positions, or from completion of one exercise to the starting position of the next?
4. Is the measurement the straight-line distance between those points, or the distance along the intended handler path? How is it measured after a turn or diagonal?
5. Do these distances apply from Start to the first exercise and from the last exercise to Finish? Do they differ by level, Brace, Team, or virtual trials?

## 2. Exercises needing extra working space

6. Our temporary design estimates favor 15 feet after fast-pace signs and around selected multi-step exercises. **This is our assumption, not an interpretation we regard as confirmed.** Which of the following actually require additional space, on which side of the sign, and how much?

7. Where a sign specifies handler steps rather than feet, what distance should a designer reserve? Should the footprint include allowance for the permitted initial backward steps when calling the dog to front?
8. Are the template icons suitable for measuring required exercise footprints, or are their sizes only approximate illustrations? Is there an authoritative footprint table?

## 3. Companion signs

9. The Trial Officials Guide places a Call Dog sign **5–7 feet** from its paired station. Is that measured sign to sign, and in what direction? Does it apply to all relevant left- and right-sided combinations? How much distance is required **after Call Dog before the next station**?
10. The checklist places signs **216–219 / 519–522 5–7 feet back from 215 / 518**. Which physical points define that distance? What distance applies when the finish/forward companion follows **214 / 517** instead?
11. What spacing applies to **212→213 and 515→516**, and to **306→307–310 and 530→531–534**? Are all of these exceptions to ordinary 10/15-foot spacing?

## 4. Cones and obstacles

12. The checklist requires **10 feet from the last cone, weave pole, or obstacle to the next station**. Is this measured from the equipment's centre, nearest edge, exit face, or the point where the team completes the exercise? For spirals and Figure 8s, which cone is the “last” cone when the exit direction varies?
13. Is there a required clear approach distance **before** cone exercises, weave poles, or obstacle send zones, in addition to the send-zone dimensions?
14. Does the **3-foot obstacle clearance** apply to the complete physical equipment, including jump wings and tunnel bags, or another reference point? Is it required on all sides or only laterally? How does it interact with the required handler path?
15. Please confirm which equipment points define the **3- or 6-foot lateral offset** and the **10-foot approach** in obstacle diagrams. For broad jumps, is the approach measured to the first board? What tunnel exit clearance is needed for a curved tunnel?

## 5. Clear paths, boundaries, and special formats

16. The checklist requires a **5-foot-wide clear handler path**. Is that centred on the drawn handler line, or does it extend a specified amount toward the dog? Must it remain clear of sign holders, equipment, boundaries, and other exercise footprints throughout turns as well as straight sections?
17. For **318/319/541/542**, where does the required **10-foot clearance on the backing-up side** start: the sign, the turning point, or another point?
18. For the **8-foot Start/Finish and Team transition clear paths**, where does measurement begin, and must the full 5-foot path width remain clear for that entire distance?
19. Does Brace require any additional station-to-station distance or path width beyond its specified cone/food-bowl adjustments? For Team's **15-foot waiting-area separation**, is distance measured from the waiting-area edge or from the waiting handler/dogs to the working path?

Thank you for helping us make the course layouts accurate and useful.
