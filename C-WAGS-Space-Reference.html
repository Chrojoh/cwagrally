C-WAGS: space & approach audit

Reviewed source editions · 14 October 2026 · not a certification of complete compliance
A sign-to-sign gap is not the whole exercise footprint. Numeric equipment spacing, a dog's retained position, handler travel and turning room need separate checks. No universal fixed rectangle was found for every exercise. Existing app rectangles are chosen design allowances; they are not published minimum safe areas. The reviewed PDFs have 2012/2013/2021 edition labels; current-edition status has not been confirmed.
Ordinary separation

The guidelines recommend at least 10 ft, with joined/stated-distance exceptions. The app uses station anchors and enforces a conservative minimum; this does not prove working clearance.

Guidelines, p12
Approach & sign location

General guidance places signs to the right; direction-changing exercises use signs in front. Equipment and joined exercises have exceptions. Anchor, sign, equipment and dog positions must not be conflated.

Guidelines, p12
Ring area

Starter / Advanced / Zoom 1 / 1.5: 1,800–3,500 sq ft. Pro / ARF / Zoom 2: 2,400–4,000 sq ft. Area alone cannot establish a usable arrangement.

Confirmed gaps and corrections
  • P16 was mislabelled. Corrected to Turn–Send Over Jump and excluded from generation until its actual jump/recall geometry is represented. Manual use produces a blocking validation issue.
  • P17/P18 now check 8–18 ft for a direct Leave Dog → recall pair. A sign-anchor measurement still assumes the left dog's position; intervening equipment chains need a retained dog-position model.
  • A21/A22 net exit headings corrected to right/left 90°. P21 corrected to forward travel.
  • R16 single-switch versus Zoom 2 double-switch remains unresolved; automatic selection disabled.
  • R12 companion and R17 freestyle are not fully represented.
  • Most ordinary turns, backing, fronts and walk-arounds lack swept-area checks. Green rule checks must not be read as full clearance approval.

What is still needed to validate working room

Each station needs distinct sign, handler, dog and equipment anchors; incoming and outgoing paths; dog/handler body clearances; selected equipment dimensions; entry/exit corridors; and a swept path that includes lateral movement and return travel. The chosen clearance profile must remain labelled as a design assumption wherever the rules do not prescribe a number.

Zoom inherits exercise dimensions from its allowed source signs; its special variants require level-aware handling. It does not provide a new universal footprint.
